Why this exists

A quick founder note—so you know exactly what this is (and what it’s not).

I built Hive & Honey Mentorship because too many new beekeepers quit—not from lack of effort, but from feeling alone when something went wrong.

When you open a hive and something looks “off,” the internet can’t see your frames, doesn’t know your local conditions, and won’t give you a clear next step. This is structured support that helps you move from confusion → confidence, one decision at a time.

FAQ

Quick answers to reduce hesitation and increase signups.

What counts as “simple” vs “detailed” Q&A?

Simple: short yes/no guidance like “Is this normal?” “Should I feed?” “Is this swarm prep?”
Detailed: hive log review, treatment plans, recovery troubleshooting, or multi-step planning.

Do you offer emergency support?

No emergency guarantee. Phone/video support is scheduled. Email support is typically 24–48 hours.

Is this hive management?

No—this is mentorship and education. You keep full control of your hive.
